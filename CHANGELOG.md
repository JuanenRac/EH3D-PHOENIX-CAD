# Changelog

All notable changes to this project are documented here.

## [Unreleased]

- Drag holes and posts with the mouse in the 3D view and in the plan: a press within `hit_radius` pixels of the drawn centre of a hole or a post picks it (the nearest to the pointer, and of two at the same distance the nearer to the viewer) and selects it in the tree, the drag moves it along the plane of its face (the plane of the floor for a post) keeping its place under the pointer, the position snaps to the grid of the settings (a check box in the window turns the snapping on and off) and the new position is kept only where the model accepts it: the margins, the distances to the other holes and posts, the lid and every other check are the ones of the model, a refusal is told in the status bar with its code and the thing stays where it last was accepted. The whole drag is one entry of the history that undo takes back to where it was picked, Esc gives it up, and a face seen edge on (the front face from above) cannot be dragged in that view and the view is turned or moved instead.
- Add the way back from a point of a view to a point of a plane of the model (`eh3d_phoenix_cad::projection`, with the lines and the camera moved there from `scene`, which still exports them): the mapping of a view goes both ways, exactly the same one that draws the lines, with the zoom and the movement of the view, and says so when the plane is seen edge on; `eh3d_phoenix_cad::drag` has the snapping in integer nanometres (half away from zero), the offset that keeps a thing under the pointer, the choice of the nearest thing within a radius and the distance of a point to a segment.
- Measure distances and angles in the 3D view and in the plan: two tools in a panel above the status bar. A distance is read between two points or edges (the centres of the holes and of the posts, the corners and the edges of the outer box and of the inner space) as the shortest line between them, with its steps along x, y and z; an angle is read between two edges (the acute one) or at the second of three points. A click picks the point within `pick_radius` pixels (points first, then edges), the status bar says how many are still needed, the reading goes to the status bar and to the panel, the line and a cross on each point are drawn in the views, the panel keeps the newest `keep` readings and a button clears them.
- Add the measurements as a library module (`eh3d_phoenix_cad::measure`, tested without a screen): the references (a point or an edge with a label that the window turns into words), the nearest points of a point and an edge and of two edges (parallel, skew, crossing and past the ends), the angle from the arctangent of the cross and the dot products so that it is exact near 0 and 180 degrees, and the refusal of an edge or a leg with no length.
- Read the measurements in the unit of the settings or in one of their own (`unit = display`, `nm`, `um`, `mm`, `cm`, `m`, `in` or `mil`) with their own decimals for lengths and for angles; lengths are rounded to the nanometre and written by the same exact code as the rest of the program.
- Add the settings of dragging and measuring to the settings of the window (`[drag]` and `[measure]` in `phoenix-cad-window.default.cfg`, which the `config` command writes): whether the grid is on when the window opens, the grid (0 mm is none), the radius of a press, the unit and the decimals of a reading, the radius of a click, how many readings are kept and the size of the cross drawn on a point; every value is checked when the file is read and the shipped file is the one the tests read.
- Add the codes `PHOENIX-CAD-038` (measurement not valid) and `PHOENIX-CAD-039` (drag not possible), with their text in the eight languages, and the texts of the new tools, readings and hints in the eight languages (Portuguese of Portugal).
- Add 34 tests with values worked out by hand and checked again with a second calculation: the mapping of the plan and of the 3D view in both directions (pixels per millimetre, movement, zoom, a face seen edge on), the snapping on both sides of zero and at a tie, a drag that keeps its place under the pointer, a post that lands on 30.5 and 20 mm from the pointer at 30.3 and 19.8, a position refused on top of another post or outside the walls, a drag that is given up or never moved, a hole dragged in the 3D view and not in the plan, the distance of 13 from the steps 3, 4 and 12, of 5 between parallel edges and 4 between skew ones, the distances of the example (sqrt 4580 = 67.676 mm, also 2.6644 in), the angles of 45, 90, 180, 0 and arccos of one over the root of three, the readings kept and the settings.
- Bring the documentation up to date with the code: the status and the scope of the window, the table of the codes, the header of the library, the eight README files (the description of the window and the counters: 493 tests, 490 of the Rust library (474 tests, 13 of the program and 3 examples) and 3 of the base).

## [0.0.4] - Sketches with a constraint solver and window, extrusion and pocket, sketches stored in the project, and synchronised documents

- Add the window of the program (`eh3d-phoenix-cad`, built with `--features gui`, with the icon of the program, the Forge theme and the same exit codes as the other programs of the suite): a tree of the model (the box, its holes, posts, edges, the lid and its screws, and the envelope that is checked), a panel of properties, a 3D view, a plan and a status bar, with the language chosen in the window and dropped files opened.
- Edit the box from the properties: a length, a variable name or a formula for each dimension (with the value it calculates to shown beside it), and the name, face, position, diameter, height and bore of each hole and post, the treatment of the edges and the screw hole, clearance and lip of the lid. The model is built again with the new value and the change is kept only when the model accepts it; a refused value is told with its code in the language of the window and leaves the project as it was.
- Check the fit again after every change: the board of the project (or, without one, an envelope typed in the properties) is compared with the inner space with the clearance of the printer profile, and the status bar says whether it fits, with the room left on each axis, or whether it fits turned a quarter of a turn; it also shows the outer and inner sizes, the volume of the material and how many steps the model refused.
- Add the history of the changes of the window (`eh3d_phoenix_cad::history`, depth from the `undo.depth` setting): undo and redo by name from the buttons and from the keyboard, forgetting what could be redone when a new change is made; steps can be suppressed and brought back from the properties, and the change is part of the history too.
- Add the 3D view and the plan (`eh3d_phoenix_cad::scene`): the lines of the outer box, the inner space, the plane of the lid, the holes (the opening on the face and the bore), the posts with their bore and foot, the screw holes of the lid and the board, turned with the mouse (drag), zoomed with the wheel and reset with a double click; the plan looks straight down and moves with the mouse; the selected item of the tree is drawn on top in its own colour.
- Add the picture mode of the window: `--shot FILE.png` with `--size`, `--theme` (forge, dark or light), `--wait`, `--lang`, `--open`, `--config` and `--replace`; it keeps no settings and exits with 0 when the file was written, 1 when it was not and 2 when the arguments are wrong.
- Add the `check` command: it reads a project, builds the box and prints the measures, the fit and the state of every step in the language asked for, and exits with 1 when a step was refused or the board does not fit; add the `config` command, which writes every setting of the library and of the window with its default value.
- Add the settings of the window to a file outside the code (`phoenix-cad-window.default.cfg`, read with `--config FILE` over the defaults, in the same file as the settings of the library if wanted): the size of the window, the point of view, the limits and speeds of the zoom and the turn, the segments of a circle, the box a new project starts with, the envelope checked without a board and the size given to a new edge treatment.
- Add the texts of the window in the eight languages of the suite (`eh3d_phoenix_cad::lang`, with Portuguese of Portugal), with tests that every sentence has its text in every language and the same places to fill; the diagnostics keep the texts of `messages`.
- Add an example project (`examples/box.phoenixproj`: a box with two holes, rounded corners, four posts under the mounting holes of a board, a lid screwed onto them and the outline of the board) that the tests of the window and of the `check` command read, and keep project files byte for byte on every system with `.gitattributes`.
- Lint the window in the CI (`cargo clippy --all-targets --features gui`) and install its libraries on Linux; add the icon and the version information to the Windows program.
- Add the sheet of variables to the window: a row for each variable with the value or formula to type (a length with or without its unit, or a formula over the other variables and the constants of the settings, which is calculated once), what it calculates to and the parameters of the box that depend on it, directly or through other parameters; adding, changing and removing a variable are entries of the history, every parameter that uses it is calculated again, and a value the model refuses, a name that is taken or a variable that a parameter still uses leaves the project as it was.
- Add and remove steps from the window: buttons add a hole, a post or the edge treatment at the tip of the history and select it, and remove the selected step; a new hole or post starts where the settings say and moves along by a pitch until the model accepts it, a step that a later step needs is not removed, and each addition or removal is one entry of the history that undo and redo take back and forth.
- Add the defaults of a new step to the settings of the window (`[new_step]` in `phoenix-cad-window.default.cfg`): the number of positions tried and the pitch between them, the face, position and diameter of a new hole, and the position, diameters, height and kind of bore of a new post, checked when the file is read.
- Add the texts of the sheet of variables and of the buttons in the eight languages, and tests with values worked out by hand for the sheet, the formulas, the refused values, the added and removed steps, the tip and a project written and read again.
- Add the model of the 2D sketch (`eh3d_phoenix_cad::sketch`, guide in `docs/SKETCH.md`): points, lines, circles, arcs, polylines and rectangles (four points, four lines and the constraints that keep the sides horizontal and vertical) on a plane that is a face of the box with an origin and an offset, with coordinates in integer nanometres like the rest of the model, construction geometry, identifiers that are counters inside the sketch (`e1`, `c1`) and are never given again, and elements that are checked when they are added.
- Add the constraints of the sketch: coincident, point on a line, circle or arc, horizontal, vertical, parallel, perpendicular, tangent, equal, symmetric about a line or a point, midpoint and fixed, and the dimensions distance, horizontal and vertical distance, length, radius, diameter and angle; a constraint is checked for the kinds of elements it ties, can be turned off without being removed, and a dimension can follow an expression over the variables of the project and the constants of the settings, calculated all or nothing.
- Add the solver of the sketch (`sketch::solver`, no external dependency): damped least squares over the residuals of the constraints with a Jacobian by central differences, which moves the sketch only when it finds a solution (rounded to the nanometre) and reports the degrees of freedom left, the constraints that repeat or contradict each other with the earlier constraints they depend on, or that it did not converge; it is deterministic bit for bit, and a rectangle of 40 by 25 mm held by its centre comes out on exact coordinates.
- Add the settings of the sketch to a file outside the code (`phoenix-cad-sketch.default.cfg`): the tolerance, the steps, the largest move, the step of the differences, the rank tolerance, the damping, the stall limit and the limits of a sketch and of its file, checked when the file is read, in the same file as the other settings if wanted.
- Add the sketch file (`.phoenixsketch`, `sketch::file`): one sketch in plain text with a version of its own, kept next to the project file without changing the format of the project file, written atomically with numbered copies, read under a size limit and refused with its line when it is of a newer version, not understood or does not hold together.
- Add the codes `PHOENIX-CAD-031` to `PHOENIX-CAD-035` (element, constraint, over-constrained, not converged and sketch file) with their text in the eight languages, and 32 tests with values worked out by hand and checked again with a second calculation: exact coordinates of a rectangle, a circle tangent to two lines, two circles tangent from outside and from inside, an angle, a perpendicular, parallel and equal lines, symmetry, a midpoint, an arc, the degrees of freedom, a repeated constraint, two lengths for one line, a sketch that cannot close, running out of steps, the same bits on every run, the file and the settings.
- Add the state of the sketch window (`eh3d_phoenix_cad::sketch_ui`, tested without a screen): the tools (select, point, line, polyline, rectangle, circle and arc, and a switch for construction geometry), snaps to the points, lines and grid with the point on object and coincident constraints they imply, the lines made horizontal or vertical when they are drawn near an axis, the selection of elements and constraints, the constraints and dimensions that fit the selection, dragging with the solver running at each move, and undo, redo and the sketch file.
- Add the health of the sketch to the window: the degrees of freedom left, a green, amber or red indicator for fully constrained, under-constrained and over-constrained or unsolved, the lists of the constraints that repeat or contradict the others (kept so that they can be seen, switched off one by one or all at once, the latest first) and the list of the elements.
- Add dimensions that take a value, a variable of the project or a formula over the variables and the constants (`width / 2 + 2 mm`) and are edited in place; the dimensions that use a variable follow it when the variable changes, and a text the model refuses leaves the sketch as it was.
- Add the sketch to the window (a Sketch part beside the Box part): a canvas with a grid, axes, zoom with the wheel and panning, a toolbar, a panel of constraints, a panel of dimensions, a status bar with the indicator and a menu on the right click that offers the constraints and dimensions that fit the selection; a sketch file opens it directly, `--view model|sketch` chooses the part that shows first, the picture mode takes it, and an example sketch of a plate with two holes (`examples/box.phoenixsketch`) is fully constrained.
- Add the settings of the sketch window to the file of the sketch (`[sketch_ui]` in `phoenix-cad-sketch.default.cfg`: the grid, the radius of a snap, the angle that counts as horizontal, the limits of the zoom, the segments of a circle and more), checked when the file is read; the `config` command now writes the settings of the solver and of the sketch window too; add the texts of the sketch window in the eight languages and 39 tests with values worked out by hand and checked again with a second calculation.
- Add the profile of a sketch (`eh3d_phoenix_cad::profile`): the closed outlines of the lines, arcs and circles that are not construction geometry, joined end to end within a tolerance, checked for being closed, for not crossing or touching themselves or each other (exact in integers) and for a least area, and sorted by containment into regions and holes, counter-clockwise the outer ones and clockwise the holes; the errors say where, in millimetres, with the new code `PHOENIX-CAD-036`. A rectangle of 40 by 25 mm with a hole of diameter 6 mm raised 10 mm gives 40 * 25 * 10 - pi * 3^2 * 10 mm3, worked out by hand and checked again with a second calculation.
- Add the steps `Extrude` (adds material outwards from the face of a sketch) and `Pocket` (cuts inwards to a depth or through the face) and `RemoveFeature`, as operations with an exact inverse: the outline is taken from the sketch every time the box is built, must lie inside the face (inside the margin of the edges for a pocket) and a pocket may not be deeper than the material; they are checked again when the parameters change, their volume goes to the material (marked as not exact), and the STEP export and the model for fabrication refuse them with `PHOENIX-CAD-022` while the script says in a comment that it does not draw them. New code `PHOENIX-CAD-037`, with its text in the eight languages.
- Keep the sketches inside the project file, in a new optional domain `sketch` with its own version (`sketch::domain`), as the owner decided: the same envelope, a project without sketches is the file it was before, a program that does not know the domain keeps it byte for byte, and a domain that is damaged or of a newer version opens the project read-only and is never written over. The recipe is written as version 2 of the domain `mechanical` only when it has an extrusion or a pocket and as version 1 otherwise, so old projects open as before and the two spellings cannot be mixed. The sketch file `.phoenixsketch` stays as the way in (`Window::import_sketch_file`) and out (`Window::export_sketch_file`); the migration and the compatibility are written in `docs/PROJECT_DOMAIN.md` and `docs/SKETCH.md`.
- Add extrusions and pockets to the window: buttons that add them, the group *Extrusions and pockets* in the tree, the sketch and the height or depth (or *through*) in the properties, the volume in the status bar, their outline in the 3D view and the plan, and in the sketch part a box for the name and the button *Keep in project*; the new steps start with `extrude_height` and `pocket_depth` of the `[new_step]` section of the settings of the window, and the `check` command reads the sketches of a project, takes the new settings of the sketch from the file given with `--config` and prints the new steps.
- Add the settings `profile_join`, `profile_min_area`, `profile_chord` and `max_sketches` to the sketch settings, an example project with a sketch and an extrusion (`examples/plate.phoenixproj`) and 45 tests with values worked out by hand: the areas and volumes above, a half disc and a slot made of arcs, islands, outlines that are open, branch, cross, touch, fold back or are too small, every refusal of a step, the round trip of the recipe and of the project, a project written before the sketches, a domain of a newer version and a damaged one kept as they are, the view lines and the command.
- Bring the documentation up to date with the code: the guides of the sketch and of the project domain, the codes, the status, the ideas left for later, the scope, the requirements, the dependencies, the decisions and the threat model, the headers of the modules, the help of the program, the Architecture and Repository Structure pictures the manifest and the description of the crate, and the eight README files (the counters, the description of what runs, the stack and the build, the status table, the quick start and the roadmap).
- Keep the test counter of the README files up to date: 459 tests, 456 of the Rust library (440 tests, 13 of the program and 3 examples) and 3 of the base.

## [0.0.3] - Parametric expressions, rounded edges, special holes, mass properties, posts and a screwed lid, and the mechanical domain of the project

- Add posts and a screwed lid (`eh3d_phoenix_cad::mount`): posts that rise from the floor under the mounting holes of a board, with a bore for a threaded insert or for a self-tapping screw and an optional conical foot, made by `posts_for_board` from the outline and the mounting holes of a board and the table of posts of the settings (new `[fixing.<screw>]`, `[posts]` and `[lid]` sections); the new operations `AddPost`, `RemovePost`, `ReplacePost` and `SetLid`, each with an exact inverse; a lid with a through hole and a recess for the head over each post, over the four corners when the box has no posts, and an optional centring lip with the clearance of the printer profile; checks of the walls, the other posts, the holes of the floor, the height of the board and its tallest part, and the lip; the codes `PHOENIX-CAD-027` to `PHOENIX-CAD-030` with their text in the eight languages (`PHOENIX-CAD-005` now covers posts too).
- Draw the posts, the lip and the holes of the lid in the script; write the posts and the plain holes of the lid in the STEP file, and refuse with `PHOENIX-CAD-022` the conical foot of a post and the centring lip; the model for fabrication widens the holes of the lid; the mass and the moments say they are not exact when a box has posts or a lid fixing.
- Add the mechanical domain of the project file (`eh3d_phoenix_cad::mechanical`, contract in `docs/PROJECT_DOMAIN.md` and `docs/adr/0011-mechanical-domain-recipe.md`): the recipe of the box (parameters with their expressions, ordered steps with their names and suppressed flags, the tip and the interface of the board) is written in one canonical text with its own version and read again to the same box, with limits in the new `[mechanical]` section of the settings, a migration from `mechanical-parameters` and the rule that a domain of a newer version is kept byte for byte.
- Add tests with values worked out by hand for the posts, the lid, the script, the STEP file and the domain, properties over random sequences of operations (a refused one changes nothing, undoing gives back the original, a recipe written and read gives the same box) and every cut of a domain refused.
- Add the language of expressions for the parameters (`eh3d_phoenix_cad::expr`, guide in `docs/EXPRESSIONS.md`): a lexer and a parser with the usual precedence, parentheses and signs; exact rational numbers of 128 bits with every operation checked for overflow; lengths and angles with the units `nm`, `um`, `mm`, `cm`, `m`, `in`, `mil`, `deg` and `turn`; comparisons with `and`, `or` and `not`; the functions `min`, `max`, `abs`, `floor`, `ceil`, `round` (to a step), `clamp`, `sqrt` (only when exact), `hypot`, `sin`, `cos` (the integer table of angles) and `if` (which evaluates only the branch it takes); limits of length, depth, steps and exponent read from the settings; the same result every time; codes `PHOENIX-CAD-023` to `PHOENIX-CAD-026`, with the column of the problem in each message and the text in the eight languages.
- Add `eh3d_phoenix_cad::parametric`: a parameter of the enclosure can be a fixed length, a variable or an expression that names variables, the constants of the settings and other parameters. Each variable and parameter is an object of the graph of dependencies, so a definition that depends on itself is refused with `PHOENIX-CAD-013` and the model stays as it was, and a change recalculates only the parameters that depend on it (`ParamModel::resolve_with`).
- Add the constants of the settings for expressions: the sizes of the table of screws (`screw_M3_clearance`), the compensation of the printer and the keys of a new `[constants]` section, with the new `[expr]` section for the limits of the language; the names `and`, `or`, `not`, `true`, `false` and the units can no longer be the name of a variable.
- Write the parameters in the project file, in the new critical domain `mechanical-parameters` (`Document::set_parameters` and `Document::parameters`): the base values, the variables and the definition of each parameter, with expressions in canonical form. A file without the domain opens as before and a program that does not know it opens the file read-only.
- Add the centre of mass and the moments and products of inertia of the enclosure (`Enclosure::mass_properties`), in integers: every part of the solid is a box or a cylinder with exact moments, summed in 256-bit integers and rounded once at the end; the figure says when something was approximated (a countersink taken as a cylinder of its mean diameter, a slot as the rectangle that holds it, the treatment of the edges left out); code `PHOENIX-CAD-009` when a result does not fit.
- Draw the new shapes in the script: countersunk, counterbored, rectangular and oval holes, and rounded and chamfered edges cut along their whole length; add `step::export_step`, which writes the STEP file and refuses with `PHOENIX-CAD-022` a model that the file does not draw (rounded or chamfered edges, holes other than straight round ones) instead of leaving the shape out.
- Add countersunk, counterbored, rectangular and oval holes (`HoleStyle`) with `Settings::screw_hole`, which takes the sizes of a screw from the table of the settings; the printer compensation widens every size across of a hole and keeps the edges; codes `PHOENIX-CAD-020` and `PHOENIX-CAD-021`.
- Add rounded and chamfered edges of the box (`Edging`: the vertical edges, the edges of the lid and the edges of the floor), with their volume, the names of the new edges and faces, the checks against the box and the holes, and the code `PHOENIX-CAD-019`.
- Update the requirements, the status and the list of ideas left for later to what the library does now, with the proposals that need a decision.
- Add `eh3d_phoenix_cad::messages`: the text of every diagnostic in the eight languages of the suite, checked against the registry of codes; add two examples of the API that run as tests and three regression tests.
- Add `eh3d_phoenix_cad::project`: the envelope of a project file with a format version, named domains that are kept byte for byte when this program does not know them, read-only opening when a critical domain is not understood, a chain of migrations from older versions and a size limit; code `PHOENIX-CAD-018`. What a domain holds is left to the contract of the mechanical domain.
- Add `eh3d_phoenix_cad::datum`: reference frames with an origin and one of the 24 axis-aligned rotations, exact in integers, and the frame of each face of the enclosure, which places a point of a face where the script places a hole.
- Add `eh3d_phoenix_cad::vars`: named variables for the master dimensions, the binding of the parameters of an enclosure to them, families of variants (small, medium, large) and a hash of the parameters for the graph; code `PHOENIX-CAD-017`.
- Add `eh3d_phoenix_cad::graph`: a graph of dependencies between objects with a fixed order of calculation, cycles refused, a recalculation that only does what changed (with saved results keyed by the parameters and the inputs), failures that pass to the dependents without stopping the rest, and a Graphviz dump; codes `PHOENIX-CAD-013` to `PHOENIX-CAD-016`.
- Add `eh3d_phoenix_cad::pattern`: rows, grids and circles of holes (vent grids, screw circles) with exact integer positions, and `Enclosure::apply_all`, which applies a batch of operations all or nothing and returns the operations that undo it.
- Add `eh3d_phoenix_cad::mass`: the volume of the material, the volume of the holes (marked as not exact when a hole reaches a corner, the floor or the lid) and the mass for a density, in integers, with a table of densities in the settings file.
- Add `eh3d_phoenix_cad::store`: saving a file through a temporary file that is flushed and renamed into place, with as many numbered copies as the settings say, and loading a file under a size limit; code `PHOENIX-CAD-012`.
- Add `eh3d_phoenix_cad::config` and `eh3d_phoenix_cad::printer`: the settings file with its shipped defaults (copies kept, undo depth, unit shown, printer compensation and a table of metric screws, all outside the code) and the model for fabrication whose holes are widened by the profile of the printer; code `PHOENIX-CAD-011`.
- Add `eh3d_phoenix_cad::body`: the recipe of an enclosure as a body with a tip, steps that can be suppressed, the state of each step (valid, suppressed, failed with its code, after the tip) and an undo and redo history in named transactions that are all or nothing.
- Add `eh3d_phoenix_cad::names`: stable names for the faces, the edges and the walls of the holes of an enclosure, built by rule so that they do not change when other operations are added, removed or reordered, and the code `PHOENIX-CAD-010` for a name that no longer exists.
- Add `eh3d_phoenix_cad::units`: angles in integer nano-degrees with integer sine and cosine, counts and ratios with their own range, rounding half away from zero, an integer square root, and lengths read and shown in mm, um, in and mil (`1.6 mm`, `62 mil`, `1/16 in`); codes `PHOENIX-CAD-006` to `PHOENIX-CAD-009`.
- Add `eh3d_phoenix_cad::step::to_step`: the enclosure as a STEP file (a boundary representation with the inner space as a void and a cylindrical face for each hole), the same text for the same model, tested for its structure (header, references, counts); no CAD program has opened it.
- Keep the test counter of the README files up to date: 259 tests, 256 of the Rust library (253 tests and 3 examples) and 3 of the base.

## [0.0.1] - The base of the repository

- **The base that every repository of the suite has:** the manifest, the README files in eight languages, this changelog, the CI workflow, the issue and pull-request templates, and the shared checks of EH3D-DOCS (the repository check and the drawers of the two README pictures).
