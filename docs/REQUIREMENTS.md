# EH3D-PHOENIX-CAD: requirements

This register is the single source of the state of each capability. Status values: planned, prototype, implemented, validated. Six requirements (FCAD-001, FCAD-003, FCAD-004, FCAD-005, FCAD-006 and FCAD-007) are `prototype` and the other one is `planned`: none is validated and nothing has run on a machine. High test coverage is not proof of geometric or electrical correctness, so each requirement says how it will be checked and what result is expected.

| Requirement | Description | Status | How it will be checked | Expected result | Known limits |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| FCAD-001 | Generate a simple parametric enclosure from named parameters. | prototype | automatic: dimensional reference cases | A regenerated model with the same parameters is identical. | Only the box with round holes in its faces exists, in `eh3d-phoenix-cad`, with rows, grids and circles of holes (vent grids), patterns of holes and posts as steps of the recipe, sets of cooling fins, round, countersunk, counterbored, rectangular and oval holes with the table of screws, rounded and chamfered edges, posts under the mounting holes of a board with a bore for an insert or a self-tapping screw, a lid screwed into them (or at the four corners) with an optional centring lip, a recipe with a tip and suppressible steps, variables and variants, parameters written as expressions over variables, constants and other parameters, and the volume, the mass, the centre of mass and the moments of inertia (which leave the posts, the lip and the holes of the lid out and say so); the extrusions and the pockets of the closed outlines of a sketch, raised from a face or cut into it (their volume comes from the area of the outline in floating point and is rounded; a pocket is not checked against the holes and posts it meets); no ribs and no other supports. Checked with hand-made reference cases only. |
| FCAD-002 | Export the model to STEP. | planned | automatic: re-read the export and compare dimensions | Exported solids keep their dimensions within a stated tolerance. | Still `planned`: `eh3d_phoenix_cad::step::to_step` writes the enclosure as an AP214-style boundary representation (outer box, inner space as a void, a cylindrical face for each hole, and the faces of each post as part of the void), identical for the same model, and `export_step` refuses a model with rounded or chamfered edges, with a hole that is not a straight round one (the holes of the lid fixing included), with the conical foot of a post, with the centring lip or with an extrusion or a pocket of a sketch; the tests check the header, that every reference points to an earlier entity and the counts of faces, edges and cylinders, but no program has re-read the file to compare dimensions, so it is not yet `prototype`. |
| FCAD-003 | Export the model as an OpenSCAD-style script. | prototype | automatic: golden files | Same parameters give the same script. | The script of the box, its holes, its posts and its lip is written as plain text in millimetres, the same text for the same model whatever the order of the operations; it has not been opened in OpenSCAD yet, and the placement of the holes on every face is checked against the text only; the extrusions and pockets of sketches are not drawn in it (a comment says so). The window writes it to a file from its Export menu (`eh3d_phoenix_cad::export`), tested without a screen: it never writes over a file that exists unless the box for it is ticked, nor over the project that is open. |
| FCAD-004 | Apply structured change operations coming from the board tool and report conflicts. | prototype | automatic: operation tests, including conflicting ones | A rejected operation leaves the model unchanged. | Operations on holes, posts, patterns of them, cooling fins, the lid fixing, the edges, the parameters and the extrusions and pockets of sketches exist, each with an exact inverse, batches apply all or nothing, a refused one leaves the model equal to what it was, and a recipe keeps an undo and redo history in named transactions; the recipe is written in the mechanical domain of the project format and read again to the same box (`docs/PROJECT_DOMAIN.md`), but the operations do not come from the board tool yet because the board tool does not read that domain. |
| FCAD-005 | Heat-sink model with declared dissipation assumptions for the thermal lab. | prototype | automatic plus review | Every assumption is stored with the model. | A set of cooling fins (how many, how thick, how tall, how far apart, where they run) is a step of the recipe, checked against the limits of the settings, the edges and the holes of the face and the other sets, with its surface and an estimate of the heat it gives to still air (`eh3d_phoenix_cad::fins`). The assumptions of the estimate (one coefficient of natural convection for the whole surface, the conductivity of the fins, the temperature of the base over the air, fins of constant section, no radiation, no interference between fins) are written in the header of the module, in `[thermal]` of the settings and in `docs/STATUS.md`, and the window and the report call the figure an estimate; they are **not** stored in the project file, nothing is exchanged with the thermal lab, and the estimate has been compared with no measurement and no simulation. Checked with hand-made reference cases only. |
| FCAD-006 | Mechanical coordinates follow their own range rule, not capped by the board limit. | prototype | automatic: property tests | A chassis larger than a board is representable. | Lengths in integer nanometres below 2^40 (about 1100 m) against 2^30 for the board; checked with unit tests, not with property tests. |
| FCAD-007 | Catalog of parts for chassis (profiles, screws and brackets) placed in the model, with a bill of materials, a cut list, volume and mass. | prototype | automatic: hand-calculated reference cases (volumes, masses, orientations, bills) | The same model gives the same figures on every system; a part that does not fit the limits is refused with its code and the model is left as it was. | Libraries are plain text files and a placed part is a step of the recipe (`eh3d_phoenix_cad::catalog`, `docs/CATALOG.md`), written as version 4 of the mechanical domain only when a part is placed. The libraries of the examples are made up, no part is compared with a real one, the shapes have no thread, chamfer or radius, the overlap check compares bounding boxes, and the STEP file does not draw the parts. Checked with hand-made reference cases only. |

Rules: a claim of correctness names the corpus, the tolerance and the tool version it holds for. A phase is complete when the measured rate does not drop, every failure is classified and fixed or documented, and the measured figure is reported.
