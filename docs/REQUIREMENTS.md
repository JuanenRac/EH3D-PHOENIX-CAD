# EH3D-PHOENIX-CAD: requirements

This register is the single source of the state of each capability. Status values: planned, prototype, implemented, validated. Four requirements (FCAD-001, FCAD-003, FCAD-004 and FCAD-006) are `prototype` and the other two are `planned`: none is validated and nothing has run on a machine. High test coverage is not proof of geometric or electrical correctness, so each requirement says how it will be checked and what result is expected.

| Requirement | Description | Status | How it will be checked | Expected result | Known limits |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| FCAD-001 | Generate a simple parametric enclosure from named parameters. | prototype | automatic: dimensional reference cases | A regenerated model with the same parameters is identical. | Only the box with round holes in its faces exists, in `eh3d-phoenix-cad`, with rows, grids and circles of holes (vent grids), round, countersunk, counterbored, rectangular and oval holes with the table of screws, rounded and chamfered edges, posts under the mounting holes of a board with a bore for an insert or a self-tapping screw, a lid screwed into them (or at the four corners) with an optional centring lip, a recipe with a tip and suppressible steps, variables and variants, parameters written as expressions over variables, constants and other parameters, and the volume, the mass, the centre of mass and the moments of inertia (which leave the posts, the lip and the holes of the lid out and say so); the extrusions and the pockets of the closed outlines of a sketch, raised from a face or cut into it (their volume comes from the area of the outline in floating point and is rounded; a pocket is not checked against the holes and posts it meets); no ribs and no other supports. Checked with hand-made reference cases only. |
| FCAD-002 | Export the model to STEP. | planned | automatic: re-read the export and compare dimensions | Exported solids keep their dimensions within a stated tolerance. | Still `planned`: `eh3d_phoenix_cad::step::to_step` writes the enclosure as an AP214-style boundary representation (outer box, inner space as a void, a cylindrical face for each hole, and the faces of each post as part of the void), identical for the same model, and `export_step` refuses a model with rounded or chamfered edges, with a hole that is not a straight round one (the holes of the lid fixing included), with the conical foot of a post, with the centring lip or with an extrusion or a pocket of a sketch; the tests check the header, that every reference points to an earlier entity and the counts of faces, edges and cylinders, but no program has re-read the file to compare dimensions, so it is not yet `prototype`. |
| FCAD-003 | Export the model as an OpenSCAD-style script. | prototype | automatic: golden files | Same parameters give the same script. | The script of the box, its holes, its posts and its lip is written as plain text in millimetres, the same text for the same model whatever the order of the operations; it has not been opened in OpenSCAD yet, and the placement of the holes on every face is checked against the text only; the extrusions and pockets of sketches are not drawn in it (a comment says so). The window writes it to a file from its Export menu (`eh3d_phoenix_cad::export`), tested without a screen: it never writes over a file that exists unless the box for it is ticked, nor over the project that is open. |
| FCAD-004 | Apply structured change operations coming from the board tool and report conflicts. | prototype | automatic: operation tests, including conflicting ones | A rejected operation leaves the model unchanged. | Operations on holes, posts, the lid fixing, the edges, the parameters and the extrusions and pockets of sketches exist, each with an exact inverse, batches apply all or nothing, a refused one leaves the model equal to what it was, and a recipe keeps an undo and redo history in named transactions; the recipe is written in the mechanical domain of the project format and read again to the same box (`docs/PROJECT_DOMAIN.md`), but the operations do not come from the board tool yet because the board tool does not read that domain. |
| FCAD-005 | Heat-sink model with declared dissipation assumptions for the thermal lab. | planned | automatic plus review | Every assumption is stored with the model. | Not started: no limits known yet. |
| FCAD-006 | Mechanical coordinates follow their own range rule, not capped by the board limit. | prototype | automatic: property tests | A chassis larger than a board is representable. | Lengths in integer nanometres below 2^40 (about 1100 m) against 2^30 for the board; checked with unit tests, not with property tests. |

Rules: a claim of correctness names the corpus, the tolerance and the tool version it holds for. A phase is complete when the measured rate does not drop, every failure is classified and fixed or documented, and the measured figure is reported.
