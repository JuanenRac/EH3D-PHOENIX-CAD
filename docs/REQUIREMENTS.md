# EH3D-PHOENIX-CAD: requirements

This register is the single source of the state of each capability. Status values: planned, prototype, implemented, validated. Fifteen requirements (FCAD-001, FCAD-003 to FCAD-016) are `prototype` and the other one, FCAD-002, is `planned`: none is validated and nothing has run on a machine. High test coverage is not proof of geometric or electrical correctness, so each requirement says how it will be checked and what result is expected.

| Requirement | Description | Status | How it will be checked | Expected result | Known limits |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| FCAD-001 | Generate a simple parametric enclosure from named parameters. | prototype | automatic: dimensional reference cases | A regenerated model with the same parameters is identical. | Only the box with round holes in its faces exists, in `eh3d-phoenix-cad`, with rows, grids and circles of holes (vent grids), patterns of holes and posts as steps of the recipe, sets of cooling fins, round, countersunk, counterbored, rectangular and oval holes with the table of screws, rounded and chamfered edges, posts under the mounting holes of a board with a bore for an insert or a self-tapping screw, a lid screwed into them (or at the four corners) with an optional centring lip, a recipe with a tip and suppressible steps, variables and variants, parameters written as expressions over variables, constants and other parameters, and the volume, the mass, the centre of mass and the moments of inertia (which leave the posts, the lip and the holes of the lid out and say so); the extrusions and the pockets of the closed outlines of a sketch, raised from a face or cut into it (their volume comes from the area of the outline in floating point and is rounded; a pocket is not checked against the holes and posts it meets); no ribs and no other supports. Checked with hand-made reference cases only. |
| FCAD-002 | Export the model to STEP. | planned | automatic: re-read the export and compare dimensions | Exported solids keep their dimensions within a stated tolerance. | Still `planned`: `eh3d_phoenix_cad::step::to_step` writes the enclosure as an AP214-style boundary representation (outer box, inner space as a void, a cylindrical face for each hole, and the faces of each post as part of the void), identical for the same model, and `export_step` refuses a model with rounded or chamfered edges, with a hole that is not a straight round one (the holes of the lid fixing included), with the conical foot of a post, with the centring lip or with an extrusion or a pocket of a sketch; the tests check the header, that every reference points to an earlier entity and the counts of faces, edges and cylinders, but no program has re-read the file to compare dimensions, so it is not yet `prototype`. |
| FCAD-003 | Export the model as an OpenSCAD-style script. | prototype | automatic: golden files | Same parameters give the same script. | The script of the box, its holes, its posts and its lip is written as plain text in millimetres, the same text for the same model whatever the order of the operations; it has not been opened in OpenSCAD yet, and the placement of the holes on every face is checked against the text only; the extrusions and pockets of sketches are not drawn in it (a comment says so). The window writes it to a file from its Export menu (`eh3d_phoenix_cad::export`), tested without a screen: it never writes over a file that exists unless the box for it is ticked, nor over the project that is open. |
| FCAD-004 | Apply structured change operations coming from the board tool and report conflicts. | prototype | automatic: operation tests, including conflicting ones | A rejected operation leaves the model unchanged. | Operations on holes, posts, patterns of them, cooling fins, the lid fixing, the edges, the parameters and the extrusions and pockets of sketches exist, each with an exact inverse, batches apply all or nothing, a refused one leaves the model equal to what it was, and a recipe keeps an undo and redo history in named transactions; the recipe is written in the mechanical domain of the project format and read again to the same box (`docs/PROJECT_DOMAIN.md`), but the operations do not come from the board tool yet because the board tool does not read that domain. |
| FCAD-005 | Heat-sink model with declared dissipation assumptions for the thermal lab. | prototype | automatic plus review | Every assumption is stored with the model. | A set of cooling fins (how many, how thick, how tall, how far apart, where they run) is a step of the recipe, checked against the limits of the settings, the edges and the holes of the face and the other sets, with its surface and an estimate of the heat it gives to still air (`eh3d_phoenix_cad::fins`). The assumptions of the estimate (one coefficient of natural convection for the whole surface, the conductivity of the fins, the temperature of the base over the air, fins of constant section, radiation only when the settings give an emissivity, no interference between fins) are written in the header of the module, in `[thermal]` of the settings and in `docs/STATUS.md`, and the window and the report call the figure an estimate; they are **not** stored in the project file, nothing is exchanged with the thermal lab, and the estimate has been compared with no measurement and no simulation. Checked with hand-made reference cases only. |
| FCAD-006 | Mechanical coordinates follow their own range rule, not capped by the board limit. | prototype | automatic: property tests | A chassis larger than a board is representable. | Lengths in integer nanometres below 2^40 (about 1100 m) against 2^30 for the board; checked with unit tests, not with property tests. |
| FCAD-007 | Catalog of parts for chassis (profiles, screws and brackets) placed in the model, with a bill of materials, a cut list, volume and mass. | prototype | automatic: hand-calculated reference cases (volumes, masses, orientations, bills) | The same model gives the same figures on every system; a part that does not fit the limits is refused with its code and the model is left as it was. | Libraries are plain text files and a placed part is a step of the recipe (`eh3d_phoenix_cad::catalog`, `docs/CATALOG.md`), written as version 4 of the mechanical domain only when a part is placed. The libraries of the examples are made up, no part is compared with a real one, the shapes have no thread, chamfer or radius, the overlap check compares bounding boxes, and the STEP file does not draw the parts. Checked with hand-made reference cases only. |
| FCAD-008 | Simple assemblies: parts of the catalog placed relative to each other, and a check of which parts share volume. | prototype | automatic: hand-calculated reference cases (positions of every kind of mate, chains and cycles, bounding boxes that touch or overlap by one nanometre, hollow sections, turned profiles, round heads at the corner of a bar, screws through the holes of brackets) | The same model gives the same positions and the same pairs on every system; a mate that cannot be solved is refused with its code and the model is left as it was. | Mates place a part along one axis (a gap, an alignment or a stack) and are steps of the recipe (`eh3d_phoenix_cad::assembly`, `docs/ASSEMBLY.md`), written as version 5 of the mechanical domain only when a mate exists. The interference check compares bounding boxes and then exact shapes made of boxes and cylinders in whole nanometres; a screw through the hole of a bracket is allowed, a screw that enters a profile is a conflict, a head is taken as a cylinder, two cylinders along different axes as their boxes, and the volume reported is the one the bounding boxes share. Checked with hand-made reference cases and two example projects only; no real chassis and no CAD program. |
| FCAD-009 | The history of the design is a graph of dependencies: parameters with expressions and units, operations with ordered inputs, an order of calculation that does not depend on the order of writing, a rebuild that only does what an edit touches, cycles refused and an error with a code and a text in eight languages for each node. | prototype | automatic: hand-worked histories, properties with a fixed seed (editing a parameter gives the same solids as building everything again; the order is topological and does not depend on the order of writing), damaged files, cycles | A history gives the same solids and the same bytes every time, and an edit that is refused leaves it as it was. | Only the operations `box`, `move`, `array`, `union`, `difference` and `intersection` exist; the history is not yet the source of the recipe of the box; see `docs/HISTORY.md`. |
| FCAD-010 | Booleans between solids made of prisms aligned with the axes, exact in whole nanometres with no outside library, with a canonical form of the result. | prototype | automatic: hand-worked volumes and properties with a fixed seed (every point agrees with the rule of the operation, the volumes add up, the form does not depend on the order of the operands) | Two solids are the same solid when `==` says so, and an operation beyond the limits is refused. | Only boxes aligned with the axes; no cylinder, no rounded edge; not written to STEP or to a mesh yet. |
| FCAD-011 | The numeric engine of the solver of the sketches is a crate of its own with a stable contract: unknowns, residual rows, degrees of freedom, redundant and conflicting constraints, tolerances in a configuration file. | prototype | automatic: the tests of the sketch (every kind of constraint) and the tests of the crate (random targets whatever the order of the constraints, random linear systems, the rank of random equations, the same bits every time) | The same problem gives the same answer bit for bit; a refused problem changes nothing. | Dense algebra, tried on sketches of a few hundred unknowns made by the tests. |
| FCAD-012 | The window is the docking frame of the suite: panels that can be taken away, put back, moved, floated and folded, workspaces, a menu bar and the arrangement kept in the settings file, with its words in eight languages. | prototype | automatic: the panels, workspaces, menus, the keeping of the arrangement and the mode that follows the panel on show are data tested without a screen; the drawing is linted with clippy | The factory arrangement is sound, every entry of the menus has a text in each language and a damaged arrangement gives the factory one. | The drawing of the window has never been opened on a screen. |
| FCAD-013 | Drawing sheets: views of the solids of the history (front, top, right, isometric, visible and hidden edges), dimensions tied by ids to the nodes of the history and the elements of the sketches that follow an edit of a parameter and name an orphan, layers, balloons tied to the bill of materials derived from the history with item numbers that never change, the title block from the settings, sections with hatching and a line of the cut, detail views, tolerances of ISO 286 on the dimensions, chain and ordinate dimensions placed so that they do not overlap and dragged, a table of revisions, title blocks chosen from the settings, groups of lines, buttons and dragging in the panel, and SVG, PDF and DXF files. | prototype | automatic: hand-worked views, dimensions that follow a parameter, orphans, numbering across edits, files of the same bytes, a reader with a fixed seed, the exit codes of `drawing-check` | The values of the dimensions are those of the model after every edit, the numbers of the items are stable and a damaged file is refused with a code. | `evidence=simulator`: no file has been opened in a drawing program, the views are of solids made by the tests, the table of ISO 286 has not been checked against the standard; the buttons of the panel and the dragging of the cut line, the balloons and the notes are decided in code tested without a screen, and the drawing of the window has never been opened on a screen. |
| FCAD-014 | Broken view: a long part drawn without the middle of its length, with a break mark at each side, the dimensions across it still saying the real length, and a code when the range is not valid or the view does not fit. | prototype | automatic: hand-worked views (the picture is shorter by the range less the gap, the dimension says 200 mm), properties with a fixed seed (nothing is left inside the range and what is left is what lay outside it, the squeeze keeps the order), refused ranges and views | A broken view is the same bytes every time, a range that is not valid or does not fit is `PHOENIX-CAD-061` and the file is version 2 only when it needs to be. | `evidence=simulator`: tried on bars made by the tests, no file has been opened in a drawing program; the break is along one axis of the picture and a face inside the range is drawn on the first mark. |
| FCAD-015 | Notes with a leader and a centre mark on their own layer, anchored to a point of a view or to a face of a node, with no symbol of roughness or of welding. | prototype | automatic: hand-worked sheets (the leader ends on the face, the cross has the size of the settings), orphans, refused notes, lines of a newer program kept, the reader with a fixed seed | A note follows its view or its face, one whose face is gone is reported and not drawn, and a note that is not valid is `PHOENIX-CAD-062`. | `evidence=simulator`: no file has been opened in a drawing program; the text is one line and the PDF has no glyph for the Japanese and Chinese words (the SVG and the DXF carry them; no font is embedded). |
| FCAD-016 | Free attributes of an object (key and value pairs per part of the history) with limits of number, length and characters in the settings, shown as optional columns of the bill and used to fill fields of the title block. | prototype | automatic: refused keys and values for every limit, settings that change the limits, columns of the table and of the comma separated values, a field of the title block filled and overridden, properties with a fixed seed (what is accepted is read back to the same bytes) and a damaged file | An attribute that breaks a limit is `PHOENIX-CAD-063` and changes nothing; the same sheet gives the same bytes. | `evidence=simulator`: the attributes are kept in the drawing by the id of the node, not in the history file, and nothing has been opened in a drawing program. |

Rules: a claim of correctness names the corpus, the tolerance and the tool version it holds for. A phase is complete when the measured rate does not drop, every failure is classified and fixed or documented, and the measured figure is reported.
