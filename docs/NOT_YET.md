# EH3D-PHOENIX-CAD: not yet

Good ideas that are not in the first version. Writing them here avoids implementing them early.

| Idea | Why not yet | Revisit when |
|---|---|---|
| Full parametric CAD with a general geometry kernel | The history, the sketch solver and the booleans of prisms aligned with the axes exist (`docs/HISTORY.md`); surfaces of any shape need a kernel that is not chosen (record 0014) | after a CAD program has opened the plain STEP file |
| Booleans between solids that are not boxes aligned with the axes, and the history as the source of the recipe of the box | The prism solids hold boxes only and the recipe of the box has its own domain (records 0011 and 0012) | when the boxes of the history have to carry holes of any shape |
| An embedded scripting language | Designed (record 0015: optional, off by default, sandboxed) and not implemented | when a design needs it |
| glTF with colours, materials, a hierarchy of parts and curved surfaces, and the file read back into a solid | The profile `phoenix-cad-glb/1` (`docs/HISTORY.md`) writes one mesh of flat boxes and a check reads its counts, indices, volume and bounds; no viewer has opened a file | when a viewer needs more |
| Sections along a broken plane, stacked tolerances, symbols of roughness and of welding, an embedded font in the PDF, a break along both axes at once, a break in the isometric view, attributes kept in the history file, and dragging of the views on the drawing sheets | The sheets have sections, details, hatching, ISO 286 tolerances, chain and ordinate dimensions, dimensions that are placed and dragged, revisions, title blocks, broken views, notes with a leader and free attributes (`docs/DRAWING.md`) but none of these; the table of ISO 286 is still `verified = false` until the standard itself is at hand, and a font for the Japanese and Chinese words of the PDF needs a font file or a dependency | when a design needs them |
| Finite-element or CFD coupling | Belongs to the thermal lab | after the thermal lab's second stage |
| Functions defined by the person, areas and volumes as values, a tangent and inverse trigonometric functions, and variables that hold expressions | The language of expressions is done (see `docs/EXPRESSIONS.md`) with lengths, angles, numbers and booleans; a variable is still a length. These need decisions on the units of an area, on rounding of the inverse functions and on whether a variable may depend on a parameter | when a design needs them |
| Ribs, gussets and supports that are not posts | The posts under the mounting holes of a board exist (`mount.rs`); other supports need their own shapes and checks | when a design needs them |
| Regenerating the posts when the board moves | The posts are made once from the mounting holes (`posts_for_board`) and are then ordinary steps; each keeps the id of its hole, but nothing follows a hole that moves | when the board tool writes the interface of the board |
| Tabs, snaps or a lip alone as the fixing of the lid | The lid is screwed into posts (record 0011); the centring lip is an addition to the screws, not a fixing | when a design needs a fixing without screws |
| Telling the board tool that a step of the recipe failed | The build reports the state and the code of each step, but the way it reaches the board tool is not written | when the board tool reads the mechanical domain |
| Extruding only some of the outlines of a sketch, tapered or symmetric extrusions, pockets that cross the edge of a face, and a pocket that stops at a post or a hole | A step takes the whole profile of its sketch and a pocket must lie inside the face with the margin of the edges; the other shapes need rules for what is raised, how the sides lean and what a pocket meets | when a design needs them |
| Sketches on a plane with an offset or on a face of an extrusion or a pocket | The plane of a sketch must lie on a face of the box; the faces of an extrusion have no stable names yet (`names.rs` names the faces of the box) | when the faces of the features have names that survive a change of the recipe |
| Extrusions and pockets in the STEP file, the script and the model for fabrication | The three leave them out or refuse them (`PHOENIX-CAD-022`); they need faces of any shape, not only boxes and cylinders | after a CAD program has opened the plain file |
| Several sketches shown together, a sketch with its own colour and visibility in the views | The views draw the outlines of the extrusions and pockets only; a sketch is seen in the sketch part of the window | when a design needs it |
| Mates between faces, edges or holes, joints that move, a mate for an angle that is not a quarter turn, a mate for a part that is not at right angles to the axes, and the exact shape of a turned or swept body in the interference check | A mate (gap, overlap, align, stack, offset, symmetric, turn, inset or proportion) places a part along one axis from the bounding boxes of two parts (`docs/ASSEMBLY.md`); a turn of a part is one of the 24 that keep the axes on the axes; a turned or swept body is looked at by its bounding box in the mates and the interference check, and a mate cannot turn it | when a design needs them |
| Fins that are not straight plates of constant section (pins, tapered or louvred fins, fins on a curved base), fins on a face that has an extrusion or a pocket, and the interference of neighbouring fins in the estimate of their heat (radiation from the outer envelope of the set is there when `thermal.emissivity` is above 0, and `thermal.fin_view_factor` makes the opening between two fins radiate as a cavity; the view factor between the walls of two fins, and the temperature along a fin for the radiation, are not worked out) | A set is equal straight plates; the checks leave the extrusions and pockets of sketches out, a round hole counts as the square that holds it, and the estimate has one coefficient for the whole surface | when a design needs them, and when the thermal lab has a model to compare with |
| Cooling fins in the STEP file, and in the centre of mass and the moments | The STEP file refuses a box that has them (`PHOENIX-CAD-022`) and the mass leaves them out of the centre of mass and the moments and says so; the volume counts them | after a CAD program has opened the plain file |
| Using the thermal lab for the estimate of the fins | The estimate is written in this repository from the `[thermal]` settings; the models of the thermal lab are not used and nothing is exchanged with it | when the thermal lab publishes a library that this repository can use without changes |
| Changing one position of a pattern, patterns of other things than a hole, a post or a placed part (a part is repeated as parts of its own, see `docs/ASSEMBLY.md`), mirrored patterns and a pattern of a pattern | A pattern repeats one seed along a line or round a centre and is edited as a whole (a change to one position is refused with `PHOENIX-CAD-042`) | when a design needs them |
| Several boards in one box | The interface of the board holds one outline and one set of mounting holes | when a design needs two boards |
| Rounded and chamfered edges, countersinks, counterbores, slots, the conical foot of a post and the centring lip in the STEP file | The file draws the box with straight round holes and straight posts with a plain bore only; it needs conical and toroidal faces and a closed shell around each of them, and no CAD program has opened even the plain file. Until then `export_step` refuses such a model | after a CAD program has opened the plain file |
| Posts, the lip and the holes of the lid in the mass and the moments of inertia | The volume, the mass and the moments are summed from the box and its holes; the posts, the lip and the holes derived for the lid are left out and the figure says it is not exact | when the solid has them as pieces with their own exact moments |
| Sections of profile other than a rectangle (T-slot, round tube, angle), screws with a thread and other heads, brackets with more than one hole in a leg, nuts and washers | The three kinds of part hold a few numbers and are a box, a cylinder, a cone cut off or a hexagonal prism | when a design needs them |
| Holes and slots in a profile, threads, and a screw that enters a profile without being a conflict | The interference check knows the holes of brackets only: a screw through one is a pass, and a screw that goes into a profile is reported as a conflict (`docs/ASSEMBLY.md`) | when a profile has holes of its own |
| The exact volume that two parts share and the true shape of a hexagonal or countersunk head | The volume reported is the one the two bounding boxes share; a nanometre of overlap is a conflict unless `assembly.interference_tolerance` says otherwise; every head is a cylinder (a hexagon as its circle across the flats); two cylinders along different axes are compared as boxes | when a design needs them |
| Placed parts in the STEP file, in the centre of mass and the moments and with their true shapes in the views | The STEP file refuses a model that has them (`PHOENIX-CAD-022`), the mass is a total for each line of the bill and the views draw the bounding box of each part | after a CAD program has opened the plain file |
| A library kept inside the project, the update of a placed part when its library changes, and editing a library from the window | Each step keeps a copy of its part so that a project never depends on a file; libraries are plain text edited by hand and checked with the command `library` | when a design needs them |
| Exact moments for a countersink, a slot and the treated edges | The solid is summed from boxes and cylinders; a countersink is taken as a cylinder of its mean diameter, a slot as the rectangle that holds it, and the treatment of the edges is left out, and the figure says so | when a part with a cone, a stadium or a fillet has its own exact moments |
| Rounded or chamfered edges around the lid or the floor, an extrusion, a fin or a pocket that touches the edge of its face, a hole under an extrusion, and a solid for a post that touches a wall, in the STL file | The solid is made of flat regions and walls that meet at the same vertices, never by cutting one solid out of another, so every shape must lie inside the flat part of its face and apart from the others; such a model is refused with `PHOENIX-CAD-048` (`docs/EXPORT.md`) | when the solid is made with a boolean operation on triangles that keeps the surface closed |
| A test that a body does not pass through itself or through another, and the exact union of parts that touch | The check of the STL file is of each body on its own: a closed surface with the exact volume of the model; the parts are separate solids in the file | when the solid has a boolean operation |
| A section drawn in a view and PDF in the drawings | The DXF and SVG drawings show the outline, the cavity as hidden lines, the openings of the face that the view looks at, the centre lines and the dimensions of the box and of the positions of the holes, with no arrow heads, on one view or on a sheet of three with an optional border, a legend for each view, a projection symbol and, in SVG, a paper size of the A series with a standard scale (`docs/EXPORT.md`); neither file has been opened in a drawing program | after a drawing program has opened the plain files |
| The swept body in the STEP file, a path in three dimensions or with a corner at an arc, a profile that turns along the path, a sweep along a path that crosses itself, and the exact box of a swept body | A swept body is a step of the recipe (`add-swept`, `docs/SKETCH.md`) along a flat path of lines and arcs with mitred corners between lines; its box is the one of the vertices of its triangles, the STEP file refuses it (`PHOENIX-CAD-022`), and the other paths are refused (`PHOENIX-CAD-036`) | after a CAD program has opened the plain file |
| The turned body in the STEP file, an axis that is a line of the sketch, and the exact box of a part of a turn | A turned body is a step of the recipe (`add-turned`, `docs/SKETCH.md`) with its volume, mass, centre of mass and triangles, but the STEP file refuses it (`PHOENIX-CAD-022`) and the box of a body turned by less than a full turn is the box of the full turn | after a CAD program has opened the plain file |
| Offsetting an outline that has arcs, trimming and extending at an arc or a circle, and patterns whose copies stay tied to the originals | The offset takes circles or the lines of a closed outline (the arcs of a contour are refused); a trim or an extension is made with another line; the copies of a pattern are free geometry (a circle keeps an equal radius) | when a design needs them |
| STEP files with curved faces or edges, and the STEP file of an extrusion with holes made of circles | The reader takes flat faces on planes with straight edges only (`docs/EXPORT.md`) and refuses a cylinder, a cone, a sphere, a torus, a surface of revolution or of linear extrusion, a B-spline and a circle or an ellipse as an edge, by the entity and its name; the file of a sketch raised to a prism writes every circle as a polygon; neither has been opened in a CAD program | after a CAD program has opened the plain file, and when a design needs a round hole kept as a cylinder |
| Colours, names of faces, assemblies with several placements and the units of angle in a STEP file that is read, and a DXF sheet for the section and the sketch drawings | The reader takes the solids and nothing else of a file; the DXF sheet of a paper size is made for the views of the box and for the sheet of views only | when a design needs them |
| An editing lock of the project on the server while it is open, files that are not text in the store, a login window, and a plugin `cad` on the server | The link saves and reads a project and sends reports with the tag of the version (`docs/SERVER.md`), which stops a lost update without a lock; the client of the suite sends text, so a project file that holds other bytes is refused; the commands log in with `--user` and a variable; the sheet of the plugin is written and the server has none | when the client of the suite sends bytes, when the window has a place for the server, and when the server has the plugin |
| The union, difference and intersection of two solids, and the cut of a solid by anything but a plane or a box | `mesh-clip` cuts a solid by a plane or keeps what is inside a box and closes the cut; joining two solids needs the triangles of both to be cut along the line where their surfaces cross, with a way to tell inside from outside that does not fail on touching surfaces | when a design needs a boolean operation on files of triangles |
| The limits and fits of the standard for all letters and grades, for sizes over 500 mm and for sizes under 1 mm, and a table checked against the text of the standard | `iso-fit` has the letters d, e, f, g, h, js, k, m, n and p (the holes D, E, F, G, H and JS) for IT5 to IT12 up to 500 mm, from a table that says `verified = false`; the letters a to c and r to z, the holes K to ZC with their corrections and the grades IT01 to IT4 and IT13 to IT16 are not in it | when the owner of the repository has the standard to check the table with |
| 3MF files with the production, slice and beam extensions, ZIP64 packages, 3MF materials and colours of other programs, and compressed STEP files | The 3MF reader takes the core meshes, components and transformations of the build; components that live in other files of the package, slices, beam lattices and ZIP64 are refused by name, and the colours are not read | when a design needs them |
| IGES, JT, and the curved faces of STEP and of the faceted STEP file | Faceted STEP writes each triangle as a face; no CAD program has opened it, and the surfaces of revolution and the B-spline faces of the files of other programs are refused by the reader | after a CAD program has opened the faceted file |
| Vector colours, line weights and layers of an SVG drawing, text and clones in an SVG drawing, and clipping paths | The SVG reader draws the geometry (lines, circles, rectangles, ellipses and paths) as lines and circles and counts and tells what it leaves out | when a design needs them |
| Exact moments of inertia of a solid of prisms, thickness along a slanted line, a mirror that keeps only the reflection, and a rotation that is not a quarter turn | The queries of `solidquery` are the thickness along the three axes, the surface and the centre of mass; the sums of the moments would overflow 128 bits for the largest solids, a slanted line needs more than the grid, `mirror` keeps the input beside its reflection (take the reflection with a `difference` of the input) and only quarter turns keep the boxes aligned with the axes | when a design needs them |
| A watertight STL file with no T-junction | The STL file of a history, and the OBJ, 3MF, PLY and OFF files written from the same surface, have T-junctions where rectangles of different size meet | after a slicer has opened the file |
| A round offset of a solid, a grow or a shrink that is different along each axis, and exact moments of inertia | `grow`, `shrink` and `shell` use a cube (square corners) of the same length on every axis; the tensor of inertia is floating point because the exact sums would not fit in 128 bits for a large solid | when a design needs them |
