# EH3D-PHOENIX-CAD: not yet

Good ideas that are not in the first version. Writing them here avoids implementing them early.

| Idea | Why not yet | Revisit when |
|---|---|---|
| Full parametric CAD with a constraint solver | Needs the first enclosure generator and a chosen geometry kernel | after the enclosure generator works |
| Finite-element or CFD coupling | Belongs to the thermal lab | after the thermal lab's second stage |
| Functions defined by the person, areas and volumes as values, a tangent and inverse trigonometric functions, and variables that hold expressions | The language of expressions is done (see `docs/EXPRESSIONS.md`) with lengths, angles, numbers and booleans; a variable is still a length. These need decisions on the units of an area, on rounding of the inverse functions and on whether a variable may depend on a parameter | when a design needs them |
| Ribs, gussets and supports that are not posts | The posts under the mounting holes of a board exist (`mount.rs`); other supports need their own shapes and checks | when a design needs them |
| Regenerating the posts when the board moves | The posts are made once from the mounting holes (`posts_for_board`) and are then ordinary steps; each keeps the id of its hole, but nothing follows a hole that moves | when the board tool writes the interface of the board |
| Tabs, snaps or a lip alone as the fixing of the lid | The lid is screwed into posts (record 0011); the centring lip is an addition to the screws, not a fixing | when a design needs a fixing without screws |
| Telling the board tool that a step of the recipe failed | The build reports the state and the code of each step, but the way it reaches the board tool is not written | when the board tool reads the mechanical domain |
| Extruding only some of the outlines of a sketch, tapered or symmetric extrusions, pockets that cross the edge of a face, and a pocket that stops at a post or a hole | A step takes the whole profile of its sketch and a pocket must lie inside the face with the margin of the edges; the other shapes need rules for what is raised, how the sides lean and what a pocket meets | when a design needs them |
| Sketches on a plane with an offset or on a face of an extrusion or a pocket | The plane of a sketch must lie on a face of the box; the faces of an extrusion have no stable names yet (`names.rs` names the faces of the box) | when the faces of the features have names that survive a change of the recipe |
| Extrusions and pockets in the STEP file, the script and the model for fabrication | The three leave them out or refuse them (`PHOENIX-CAD-022`); they need faces of any shape, not only boxes and cylinders | after a CAD program has opened the plain file |
| Several sketches shown together, a sketch with its own colour and visibility in the views | The views draw the outlines of the extrusions and pockets only; a sketch is seen in the sketch part of the window | when a design needs it |
| Assemblies and heat sinks in the mechanical domain | The domain keeps the recipe of the enclosure (`docs/PROJECT_DOMAIN.md`); other kinds of step need their own words in the text and a new version of the domain | when those parts are written |
| Several boards in one box | The interface of the board holds one outline and one set of mounting holes | when a design needs two boards |
| Rounded and chamfered edges, countersinks, counterbores, slots, the conical foot of a post and the centring lip in the STEP file | The file draws the box with straight round holes and straight posts with a plain bore only; it needs conical and toroidal faces and a closed shell around each of them, and no CAD program has opened even the plain file. Until then `export_step` refuses such a model | after a CAD program has opened the plain file |
| Posts, the lip and the holes of the lid in the mass and the moments of inertia | The volume, the mass and the moments are summed from the box and its holes; the posts, the lip and the holes derived for the lid are left out and the figure says it is not exact | when the solid has them as pieces with their own exact moments |
| Exact moments for a countersink, a slot and the treated edges | The solid is summed from boxes and cylinders; a countersink is taken as a cylinder of its mean diameter, a slot as the rectangle that holds it, and the treatment of the edges is left out, and the figure says so | when a part with a cone, a stadium or a fillet has its own exact moments |
